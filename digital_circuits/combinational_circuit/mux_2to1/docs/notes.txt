Dataflow style:
  assign out = sel ? b : a;
  → single line, purely combinational
  → preferred for simple MUX, arithmetic, logic
  → output must be wire

Procedural style:
  always @(*) begin
      out = sel ? b : a;
  end
  → needed when logic is complex (case, if-else chains)
  → output must be reg
  → use always_comb in SystemVerilog
  → same hardware result for combinational logic
